# Aadrik Distributors Pvt. Ltd.

# Company Policies

This document outlines the standard business policies followed by Aadrik Distributors Pvt. Ltd. These policies are intended to ensure transparency, consistency, and excellent customer service.

---

# Return Policy

Aadrik Distributors Pvt. Ltd. does **not accept returns** for products once they have been sold and delivered.

Returns will only be considered under the following exceptional circumstances:

- The product is received in a physically damaged condition.
- The product has a verified manufacturing or technical defect.

Any return request must be reported immediately upon delivery along with supporting photographs or documentation.

---

# Replacement Policy

Replacement of products is **not automatic**.

A replacement will only be approved if:

- The product is inspected by the manufacturer's authorized technical representative or technical support team.
- The defect is confirmed to be a manufacturing or technical fault.
- The manufacturer approves the replacement.

Once approved, Aadrik Distributors Pvt. Ltd. will coordinate the replacement process with the manufacturer.

---

# Warranty Policy

Unless explicitly mentioned by the manufacturer, products supplied by Aadrik Distributors Pvt. Ltd. **do not carry a company warranty**.

Any warranty applicable to a product is solely provided by the original manufacturer under their terms and conditions.

---

# Payment Terms

The standard payment policy is:

- **100% Advance Payment** before order processing and dispatch.

Any alternative payment arrangement must receive prior approval from the management.

---

# Credit Policy

Credit facilities are **not standard** and are provided only to selected customers.

Credit approval depends on factors including:

- Customer relationship
- Purchase history
- Payment track record
- Order value
- Management approval

Credit limits and payment durations are determined on a case-by-case basis.

---

# Delivery Policy

Aadrik Distributors Pvt. Ltd. strives to dispatch orders as quickly as possible.

Standard delivery commitment:

- Orders are generally delivered within **3 working days**, subject to product availability and location.
- Delivery timelines may vary due to transportation delays, stock availability, holidays, or unforeseen circumstances.

Customers will be informed promptly if any significant delay is expected.

---

# Cancellation Policy

Orders **cannot be cancelled once the invoice has been generated**.

Customers are requested to verify all order details before confirming their purchase.
